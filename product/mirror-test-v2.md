# AEGIS BEFORE v2 — Mirror Test

## Entry
**Thinking about dating?**

Before you look for someone else, let's understand you.

`BEGIN`

Privacy cue: Your reflection is yours. You can pause, skip, revise or leave.

---

## Interaction rule
One question per screen. Prefer a small set of meaningful choices plus **Tell me more**. Never force disclosure. Explain why sensitive questions are asked.

Safety/fairness rule: AEGIS explores the participant's own assumptions. It does not prescribe dating based on protected characteristics or tell the participant whom they should choose.

## MIRROR — No score

Opening:
**You gave me answers. I won't turn them into a number.**

Render a maximum of three evidence-grounded cards:

### I heard…
Neutral summary tied to the participant's answers.

### I noticed a tension…
Only when two answers genuinely pull in different directions. Phrase as a possibility: “You said X, and later Y. Those can coexist. Is there something worth looking at there?”

### I wonder…
One question, not a verdict.

Every card offers:
- That's me
- Partly
- That's not me
- Let me revise my answer

Never infer beyond available evidence. If evidence is weak say: **I don't know enough yet.**

## FINAL REFLECTION

Repeat the participant's opening statement if available.

Ask:
**Do you still think you're ready to date?**
- Yes — and I understand why better
- Maybe — there's something I want to explore first
- Not yet
- I don't know, and that's okay

Then:
**What's one thing you understand about yourself now that you didn't understand when you began?**
Optional free text / skip.

Closing:
> You came here looking for someone.
>
> We didn't show you anyone.
>
> We showed you you.
>
> What happens next is yours.

**The torch is ours. The path is yours.**

## Working Proof instrumentation
Record only non-intimate operational telemetry by default: scene reached, elapsed time, pause/skip/revise events, completion, errors, accessibility mode. Do not log answer content into analytics.

Post-experience optional measures:
1. I understand myself more clearly than before. (1–7)
2. I felt understood rather than judged. (1–7)
3. Something made me reconsider an assumption. (yes/no + optional explanation)
4. I felt in control of the experience and my information. (1–7)
5. Was anything confusing, uncomfortable or manipulative? (optional)

No effectiveness claim until real pilot evidence exists.
