# AEGIS Human Authenticity — v0.1

Product invariant: AEGIS never presents an undisclosed synthetic/machine identity as a human romantic prospect.

## KISS
- Human prospect: human identity only.
- Human Verified: human + verification passed.
- Unresolved identity: never shown as Human Verified.
- Synthetic persona: never enters the human prospect pool.
- Service assistant: permitted only as disclosed assistance, never as the romantic prospect.
- Romantic impersonation by a machine: hard block.

Verification is an identity/provenance signal, not a compatibility score and not a guarantee about character, intentions or relationship outcome.
