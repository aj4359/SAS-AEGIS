# AEGIS — Private Membership Strategy

## Strategic position
AEGIS is not a mainstream dating app and must not optimise for swipe volume, instant matches, daily engagement, or mass acquisition.

AEGIS is a private relationship service for discerning adults who value compatibility, discretion, verification, depth and patience. Membership should remain intentionally limited.

## Product promise
**Fewer introductions. Better reasons. No promise of instant matches.**

A member may wait because AEGIS should prefer no introduction to a weak introduction. Waiting is an explicit part of the proposition, not a product failure.

## Membership gate
Applicants should understand and affirm before admission:
- I value quality over quantity.
- I accept that a suitable introduction may take time.
- I do not expect an endless catalogue of profiles.
- I am willing to complete meaningful readiness and compatibility work.
- I will treat introductions with discretion and respect.
- Identity/authenticity verification is required.

Admission is selective, but must not use unlawful discrimination or protected characteristics as exclusion criteria.

## Experience principles
1. BEFORE/readiness precedes matching.
2. No swipe feed.
3. Curated introductions, not profile inventory.
4. One active introduction at a time by default.
5. Compatibility evidence should explain *why* an introduction was made without exposing proprietary scoring.
6. Sealed Reveal preserves curiosity and reduces superficial filtering.
7. Deliberate progression beats engagement addiction.
8. A member can be told “not yet” rather than receiving a low-confidence match.
9. Human safety, consent, privacy and verification override growth metrics.
10. Measure introduction quality and relationship progression, not screen time.

## Commercial model to test
Premium application/membership model; founding cohort should be deliberately small. Test willingness-to-pay before fixing final pricing. Avoid discount-led acquisition.

## Initial proof experiment
Run a 10-pair asynchronous Sealed Reveal test. Capture:
- readiness completion;
- time-to-introduction;
- acceptance/decline and reason;
- conversation completion;
- progression to voice/date;
- post-introduction quality assessment;
- safety/privacy incidents;
- whether participants felt the wait was justified.

Success is not maximum matches. Success is evidence that members prefer fewer, better-explained introductions and accept slower matching.

## Competitive guardrail
Competitors offering “one match at a time” validate scarcity but do not define AEGIS. AEGIS differentiation is the full journey: BEFORE → readiness → verification → compatibility reasoning → Sealed Reveal → deliberate progression.
