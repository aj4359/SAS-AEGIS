# AEGIS — Human Verified customer promise

**Know yourself. Know what you want. Know they are human. Then meet.**

Human Verified is an identity/provenance signal, not a character reference, compatibility score or guarantee of intentions.

AEGIS design rule:
- machines may assist reflection, safety and communication
- machines never secretly impersonate a romantic prospect
- unresolved/synthetic identities never receive Human Verified
- identity-verification evidence is kept separate from relationship/compatibility data
- raw biometric payloads are outside the AEGIS relationship record
- retain the minimum verification receipt needed to support the displayed claim

This promise is intended to be visible to customers, not hidden as an internal safety policy.
