# AEGIS — Readiness Before Matching

AEGIS is not optimized for maximum swipes or maximum synthetic conversation.

Customer path:
**self-understanding → intentions → authenticity → compatibility → consent → human introduction**

Machine assistance may guide reflection and preparation, but it does not impersonate a romantic prospect. Human Authenticity remains an independent gate rather than a compatibility score.

KISS customer promise: **Know yourself. Know what you want. Know they are human. Then meet.**
